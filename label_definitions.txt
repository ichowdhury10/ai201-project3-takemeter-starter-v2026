take: makes a basketball claim — a verdict, prediction, ranking or explanation — with no checkable fact doing the work, however calm or loud it is
reaction: responds to a game moment, a piece of news, or another poster without arguing a basketball claim of its own — a feeling, a joke, a jab, a question, or a fact shared without an argument
